Decision tree: classification

Decision tree: regression

Goal: estimate the class y of a sample from its features x1, x2. estimate a number y from a feature x.

Approach: ask a chain of yes/no questions, each about one feature: “xf ≤ t?” Every sample starts at the root and follows its answers down to a leaf, which estimates the most common classthe mean label of the training samples that land there. Training picks each question greedily: the feature and threshold t that lower the Gini impurityMSE the most.

Gini = 1 − ∑c pc2 · after = nyes n Giniyes + nno n Ginino

pc is the share of a node's samples in class c. Gini 0 means pure (one class); an even mix of two classes gives 0.5. Yes goes left, as in sklearn's plot_tree.

MSE = 1n ∑i (yi − ȳ)2 · after = nyes n MSEyes + nno n MSEno

ȳ is the mean label of a node's samples. The gray sticks are the selected node's errors: each label to the mean it is estimated by (the node's own mean, or after a split its side's). Yes goes left.

Random forest

Goal: a deep tree fits its training samples perfectly, but move a few samples and it changes a lot. Steady it by letting many trees vote.

Approach: grow T deep trees, each on its own bag: n draws, with replacement, from the n training samples (bootstrap). Each split searches only a random subset of the features (here one of the two). The forest estimates by majority vote.

ŷ(x) = the most common ŷt(x) among trees t = 1, …, T

A bag holds about 63% of the distinct samples, some more than once. The shading is the winning class, washed out where the vote is close. Click a thumbnail to see that tree alone.

Try this

  • With the root selected, click a Split button and drag the split line (or the chart below the numbers). Where is the GiniMSE after the split lowest? Compare with Best split.
  • On XOR, click Grow one level twice: the training error stays high. Now build it by hand: split the root at x1 ≤ 0, then each side at x2 ≤ 0. Why did the greedy search miss this tree?
    AnswerThe cut at 0 scores almost nothing by itself: both sides are still about half and half. Its payoff comes only from the second cut. Greedy growing scores one question at a time, so it took a cut with a slightly bigger immediate reduction, after which no second question helps much.
  • Click Grow one level until the training error is 0%MSE is near 0. Now Resample, which keeps the tree. What happens to the error, and why?
    AnswerIt jumps. The deep tree carved small leaves around single stray samples; new samples do not fall the same way. Compare a tree of depth 2 or 3.
  • Set Trees to 1: one jagged tree with islands. Slide up to 200. The boundary smooths and the testing error falls below the one-tree line, then levels off.
  • Uncheck both Bootstrap and Random feature. Why do the thumbnails all match, and why does Trees stop mattering?
    AnswerSame samples, same greedy rule: every tree is the same tree, and a vote of copies is that one tree. Randomness makes the trees disagree; the vote averages the disagreement away.
